About

Hello! My name is Thinh! I am a Postdoctoral Researcher in the Department of Aerospace Engineering at the University of Michigan, USA, working with Prof. Ilya Kolmanovsky and Prof. Jing Sun.

I received my Ph.D. from Grenoble INP, Université Grenoble Alpes, France, where I conducted research at the LCIS laboratory under the supervision of Prof. Ionela Prodan.

My research centers on constrained control solutions with low computational complexity. My applications of interest include, but not limited to mobile robotics, multicopters, electric drives, and autonomous driving.

I really like drawing, but I am not really good at it... Instead, I try follow this 'passion' by using figures, tikz, plots and illustrative demonstrations in my work! Check them out in the publication list!
